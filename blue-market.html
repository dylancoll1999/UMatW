About the Event

The Blue (Bermondsey) community is enriched by the wonderful events organized at the Blue Market place. We are pleased to be back with a song and dance event (SATURDAY 30th MAY 2026).

This special annual event will include various music ensembles, community choir, brass band, vocal performances and Salsa dancing. Each year we will pay a posthumous tribute to the late great community hero Russell Dryden who sadly passed away in May 2024.

Russell was a local champion who lived and breathed music and community enrichment at The Blue Market. Everyone is welcome to participate in the dance sessions.
