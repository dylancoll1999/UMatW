About the Event

We are delighted to announce the return of the outdoor jazz days to the renowned The Palm Tree pub forecourt located in East London's Mile End Park. The pub has been a stalwart of the jazz and swing music scene since 1977.

The outdoor jazz days are a fantastic blend of live jazz and salsa dance sessions . The salsa dance slots are hostsed by Sean Vickery from Salsa Oracle alternating in between the live jazz band sets. Each event will feature a live jazz band session featuring the host,organiser and vocalsit Stephen Pierre, providing a platform for many of London's fine music students and graduates to showcase their talents.
