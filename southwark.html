About the Event

Stephen Pierre and his jazz collective musicians will be pleased to perform back at The Band Stand in Southwark Park on Sunday 28th June 2026 between 2pm - 4pm.

This free public event is to support the worthy charity (CRY) Cardiac Risk In The Young who will be having their annual get together in Southwark Park.

Stephen Pierre is the founder of The Bermondsey Square Jazz Days and Creative Director of The Unity Music Arts Team CIC.
