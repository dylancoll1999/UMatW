About the Event

The Jam Circus in Deptford hosts our monthly jazz jam on the last Sunday of each month, from 4pm to 7pm. This regular event attracts a diverse range of musicians, who come together to perform a blend of jazz, blues, and grooves.

We extend a warm invitation to students and graduates from many of London’s music colleges to showcase their talent. The Jam Circus is an impressive venue managed as part of the Antic Inns portfolio. This gig is open to all and free entry.
