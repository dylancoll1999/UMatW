About the Event

We are delighted to return to The Union Jack (UJ) to present an afternoon of jazz, blues & grooves. The UJ was the venue which founded the idea of the UMAT jazz jams & The Bermondsey Square Jazz Days back in 2016.

The sessions presented here between 2016 and March 2020 gave valuable performance opportunities for many student musicians studying at London’s music colleges.

This special event is a re-union gathering and celebration acknowledging the success of The Bermondsey Square Jazz Days completing their 7th annual summer season. Music is a universal language and we hope to share this talent with you amongst a collective of fine musicians.
