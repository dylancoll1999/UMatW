About the Event

We are pleased to announce a new monthly Jazz & Jam session now held at The Bird in Hand Pub in Forest Hill (SE23).

Hosted on the First Sunday of each month 4pm - 7pm . We will welcome a different collective of student and graduate musicians to perform the first set, followed by a Jazz Jam hosted by Stephen Pierre. The Bird in Hand is a lovely community pub and the perfect setting to host a welcoming jazz session.

This monthly Jazz jam is proudly supported by umat.org.uk and Antic Inns. Over the past decade, many of London’s talented young musicians have gained valued experience playing at these fun jazz jams.
