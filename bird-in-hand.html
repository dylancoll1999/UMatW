About the Event

We are pleased to announce a new monthly Jazz & Jam session now held at The Bird in Hand Pub in Forest Hill (SE23).

Hosted on the First Sunday of each month 4pm – 7pm. We will welcome a different collective of student and graduate musicians to perform the first set, followed by a Jazz Jam hosted by Stephen Pierre.

This monthly Jazz jam is proudly supported by umat.org.uk and Antic Inns.
