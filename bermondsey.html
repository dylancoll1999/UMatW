About the Event

Located in the heart of one of London’s most vibrant districts, Bermondsey Square hosts our popular monthly open-air jazz sessions. Surrounded by independent cafes, restaurants, and the famous antiques market, it is the perfect spot to spend a relaxing Sunday afternoon.

We are pleased to announce the ever popular jazz days will return on the FIRST SUNDAY of each month between 2pm – 5pm. Each event is free to attend and will welcome a different collective of musicians invited by the organiser, host and vocalist Stephen Pierre
