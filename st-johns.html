About the Event

The idyllic St John’s Church Garden is an excellent venue for hosting our summer jazz days. We are thrilled to announce our 2026 return, supporting The Waterloo Festival and the local community.

This event will feature a plethora of student and gradute musicians having a fun gig and jazz jam , hosted by the organiser and vocalist Stephen Pierre with invited guest musicians. It promises to be an enjoyable and inclusive occasion for everyone to appreciate live jazz music and creative . Admission is free for all.
